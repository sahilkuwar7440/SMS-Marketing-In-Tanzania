## What Is Promotional SMS?

Promotional SMS refers to text messages used for marketing and promotional communication.

Businesses may use promotional messages to share:

* Special offers
* Discounts
* New products
* Seasonal campaigns
* Events
* Limited-time promotions
* Store announcements
* Service updates related to marketing

The message should be relevant to the intended audience and clearly communicate the main offer or information.

## Customer Segmentation

Not every customer has the same interests.

Businesses can organize their audiences based on factors such as:

* Previous purchases
* Product interests
* Location
* Customer type
* Engagement history
* Campaign preferences

For example, an online store selling different product categories could send category-specific promotions rather than sending every offer to its complete customer database.

Segmentation can make promotional communication more relevant.

## Promotional SMS vs Transactional SMS

Promotional and transactional messages serve different purposes.

| Promotional SMS         | Transactional SMS                    |
| ----------------------- | ------------------------------------ |
| Marketing communication | Service or operational communication |
| Offers and discounts    | OTP and verification                 |
| Product announcements   | Payment notifications                |
| Seasonal campaigns      | Order updates                        |
| Event promotions        | Booking confirmations                |

Keeping these communication types separate helps businesses manage their messaging strategy more clearly.

## Best Practices for SMS Marketing

A practical SMS marketing strategy can include the following:

### Keep Messages Concise

Customers should be able to understand the main message quickly.

### Make the Offer Clear

If the message contains a promotion, explain the important detail without unnecessary text.

### Use Relevant Audiences

Send campaigns to customers who are appropriate for the particular offer.

### Plan Campaign Timing

Consider when customers are most likely to engage with the message.

### Maintain Accurate Customer Data

Use up-to-date recipient information to keep campaigns organized.

### Monitor Campaign Results

Review available delivery and engagement information to understand campaign performance.
